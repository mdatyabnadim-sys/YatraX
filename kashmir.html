Safety & Precautions

  • Check weather and local travel conditions before heading out.
  • Carry drinking water, basic medicines you normally use, and a charged phone/power bank.
  • Wear suitable footwear and avoid restricted, unstable or dangerous areas.
  • Follow local authorities, guides, park rules and activity operators.

High-Altitude Travel Disclaimer

  • Older adults, pregnant travellers, infants and young children may need extra caution at altitude.
  • Watch for headache, dizziness, unusual tiredness, nausea, breathlessness or confusion.
  • Stay hydrated, avoid heavy exertion initially, and allow time to acclimatise.
  • Follow local medical and safety guidance when travelling to high-altitude areas.
